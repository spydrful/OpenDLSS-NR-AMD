# C32 strided expansion screen

Both private strided expansion variants were rejected because every measured operator was slower than the frozen Pair baseline on RX 9070 XT with AMD 26.9.1. No shipping shader, default, tuning cache or alpha6 package changed. The [scalar evidence](performance/c32-strided-expansion-v8-rx9070xt-20261003.json) binds the source, compiled modules, saved comparisons and complete timing samples.

The experiment isolated ordinary C32 FFN expansion: FLAGS24, K32, N128, no partitions or batches beyond one, E4 inputs/outputs and corrected SiLU. A wave32 gathered strided rows to retain the original publication-wave ancestry. The stream variant processed eight N16 tiles per workgroup; the tile variant processed one. Both kept two ordered K16 matrix operations with native RTE accumulator half publication, the synchronized seed load and the software Pair E4 publication helper. Contraction and graph fusion were excluded.

| Variant | Pooled operator slowdown | Range across all 21 pairs | Sum of isolated Pair medians | Sum of isolated candidate medians |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Stream |66.14–78.12% |65.87–80.34% |2.34902 ms |4.02234 ms |
| Tile |75.50–87.13% |74.51–89.69% |2.43034 ms |4.39244 ms |

Each route had five warmups followed by three pairs of 30 measured executions, in B/C, C/B, B/C order. All 2,520 samples were retained. Persistent command buffers, descriptor pools and a reusable Vulkan fence excluded allocation, image uploads, readback and CPU waits from the GPU spans. Each route used one dispatch and two ALL_COMMANDS timestamps including compute barriers. No cause is assigned to clock or thermal variation; the ordering is unbalanced. These isolated median sums are estimates, not network latency, NR-plus-bridge latency or game FPS.

The bounded suite passed 45 fixtures/315 comparisons; the target-row suite passed 47 fixtures/329 comparisons, including the repeated small fixtures. Imported weights in those suites still used synthetic inputs. A separate replay used seven fixed, saved model-generated inputs from blocks1–4 and67–69, each with 414,720 rows. Its 49 full-allocation comparisons reopened 98 raw buffers and checked 3,583,180,800 compared bytes plus 477,757,440 padding canary bytes with zero differences. The seven actual inputs, 14 inherited weight matrices and seven scale arrays were independently hashed/repacked; expansion consumed W1 only. Four odd-block output fixtures included 16 padding columns.

All 28 timing pre/post checks matched packed E4 hashes derived from those saved strict outputs. Matching production/capture E4 and a separate activated F16 trace does not reveal the original F24 pre-E4 halves. This establishes bounded byte agreement with the frozen AMD Pair route on the recorded driver; it does not establish arbitrary-input equivalence, portable arithmetic parity or original NVIDIA parity. Inputs came from a fixed generated-model capture with QKV normalization off, rather than captured game frames or evolved histories. The candidate never ran as a complete fused graph. Timing reports record validation layers disabled. Bounded and actual manifests do not record enabled-layer status. Zero reported errors do not establish enabled-layer coverage.

Separate installed-driver reports showed 46 VGPRs for both production variants and 49 for captures, 1,536 bytes of LDS and zero scratch. SGPR counts were 53/58 for stream/stream-capture and 54/59 for tile/tile-capture. Offline wave32 counts were 44/47 VGPRs; they are different observations. Each installed shader contained two static FP8 WMMA instructions and 40 hardware half casts, with no MODE setters or hardware FP8 downconversion. Static counts do not measure runtime delivery or explain the slowdown. Raw ISA text and module addresses remain private.

The public record includes only scalar identities, resource/statistical summaries and two exact copies of timing JSON: [stream](performance/c32-strided-expansion-v8-measurements/stream-ordinary-target-interleaved.json) and [tile](performance/c32-strided-expansion-v8-measurements/tile-ordinary-target-interleaved.json). NVIDIA DLLs, weights, raw tensors, images and game captures remain excluded. Both variants remain private rejected experiments. The 8 ms NR-plus-bridge and 16.67 ms complete-frame/60 real FPS targets remain unmet.
## V9 phase-mask follow-up

The separately compiled v9 variants replaced eight sequential subgroup votes with one integer phase-mask union. Their matrix operations, fragment mapping, corrected mixed3 SiLU, publication helpers and synchronization retained the v8 source. Both were rejected: every operator and every measured pair was slower than the frozen Pair baseline. The [v9 scalar record](performance/c32-strided-expansion-v9-rx9070xt-20261003.json) binds the source, saved comparisons and all timing samples.

| Variant | Pooled operator slowdown | Range across all 21 pairs | Sum of isolated Pair medians | Sum of isolated candidate medians |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Stream | 68.36–91.52% | 68.39–84.56% | 2.43166 ms | 4.23838 ms |
| Tile | 76.50–88.63% | 75.27–88.89% | 2.45006 ms | 4.42974 ms |

Fresh bounded runs passed 45 fixtures/315 comparisons and 47 fixtures/329 comparisons, including repeated small cases. Seven fixed model-generated inputs passed 49 full-allocation comparisons. Across these runs the independent audit reopened 4,609,105,920 compared bytes and checked 608,928,768 canary bytes; saved outputs also matched v8. All 28 timing pre/post checks matched canonical E4 hashes. This is bounded agreement on the recorded device and driver, not arbitrary-input equivalence or complete-model qualification.

The two timing runs retained all 2,520 samples, five warmups per route and three pairs of 30 measured executions in B/C, C/B, B/C order. The [stream](performance/c32-strided-expansion-v9-measurements/stream-ordinary-target-interleaved.json) and [tile](performance/c32-strided-expansion-v9-measurements/tile-ordinary-target-interleaved.json) reports are exact scalar copies. Their persistent command buffers, descriptor pools, reusable fence and one-dispatch GPU spans match the v8 protocol. The ordering remains unbalanced; the measured variation has no established cause. Sums of isolated medians are not complete inference or game timings.

Installed-driver reports showed 48 production VGPRs and 51 capture VGPRs, 1,536 bytes of LDS and zero scratch. SGPR counts were 34/40 for stream/stream-capture and 36/41 for tile/tile-capture. Each module had two static FP8 WMMA instructions, 40 F32-to-F16 narrowing instructions and 40 F16-to-F32 widening instructions, with no MODE setters or hardware FP8 downconversion. The earlier v8 field `hardwareHalfCasts: 40` and its prose counted narrowing instructions only; the corresponding v8 installed reports likewise contain 80 total narrowing-plus-widening instructions. The published v8 evidence and text remain unchanged. These static counts do not establish runtime delivery or explain the regressions.

V9 never ran as a complete candidate graph or on game frames and evolved histories. Timing reports record validation layers disabled; bounded and actual manifests omit enabled-layer status. No shipping code, defaults, cache or alpha6 package changed. The public files contain scalar summaries and timing samples; raw tensors, ISA text, weights, NVIDIA DLLs and images remain private.
## V10 dataflow follow-up

Three separate tile-only dataflow variants were rejected because every measured operator and every pair was slower than the frozen Pair baseline. Seed replaced the shared seed load with an opaque accumulator constructor from host-validated raw positive-zero push bits. Operand replaced shared input staging with raw global E4 bitcasts using the measured wave32 A-component map. Combined applied both changes. They retained the v9 phase-mask prefix, two ordered K16 matrix operations, corrected mixed3 SiLU and software Pair E4 publication helper. The [v10 scalar record](performance/c32-strided-expansion-v10-rx9070xt-20261003.json) binds the source and proofs.

| Variant | Pooled operator slowdown | Range across all 21 pairs | Sum of isolated Pair medians | Sum of isolated candidate medians |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Seed | 73.95–86.74% | 73.80–88.35% | 2.51024 ms | 4.48660 ms |
| Operand | 77.88–89.68% | 76.81–92.30% | 2.51112 ms | 4.57634 ms |
| Combined | 75.46–87.33% | 73.63–89.13% | 2.44188 ms | 4.38910 ms |

The bounded runs passed 45 fixtures/450 full-allocation comparisons and 47 fixtures/470 comparisons, including repeated small fixtures. A separate replay passed seven fixed model-generated inputs/70 comparisons: 5,175,705,600 compared bytes and 690,094,080 canary bytes were independently checked across 140 raw buffers. Four odd-block outputs included 16 padding columns. The audit repacked 14 inherited model matrices and seven scale arrays; expansion consumed W1 only. All 42 timing pre/post checks matched canonical packed E4 hashes from these saved outputs. Production/capture E4 and separate F40 activated-half agreement do not expose original F24 pre-E4 halves or establish arbitrary-input parity.

All 3,780 timing samples were retained under the same persistent one-dispatch protocol, with five warmups and three pairs of 30 measurements per route in B/C, C/B, B/C order. Exact scalar timing copies are available for [seed](performance/c32-strided-expansion-v10-measurements/seed-ordinary-target-interleaved.json), [operand](performance/c32-strided-expansion-v10-measurements/operand-ordinary-target-interleaved.json) and [combined](performance/c32-strided-expansion-v10-measurements/combined-ordinary-target-interleaved.json). The unbalanced ordering and measured variation have no established cause. The spans exclude uploads, readback, CPU waits and the bridge; their isolated sums are not complete inference latency or game FPS.

Installed production/capture VGPR counts were 48/51 for seed, 48/51 for operand and 49/52 for combined; SGPR counts were 36/41 for each. LDS fell to 512, 1,024 and zero bytes respectively, with zero scratch. Each installed module contained two static FP8 WMMA instructions and 40 F32-to-F16 plus 40 F16-to-F32 conversions, with no MODE setters or hardware FP8 downconversion. Lower LDS did not produce a timing win. Offline counts are separate observations and static instructions do not explain the regressions.

No contemporaneous root logs were stored for the two earlier bounded commands or the driver-info command; their manifests, source, executable and module identities are hash-bound. The later actual-input and three timing completion logs are retained. Timing reports record validation layers disabled; bounded and actual manifests omit enabled-layer status. No complete candidate graph, evolved history or game sequence was run. The variants remain private; shipping code, defaults, cache and the alpha6 package remain unchanged. Public files contain only scalar evidence and timing samples, excluding raw tensors, weights, NVIDIA DLLs, images and ISA text.
